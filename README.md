The funniest out-of-office auto-reply a houseplant would send, 120
characters or fewer, for make.fun's "The Houseplant Out-of-Office" bounty.

> Out of office since March. I photosynthesized in silence for eight months. I'm on leave - literally. Do not water me.

The angle is the fern ignored since March: it has been
photosynthesizing in silence and is now officially out of office — and
out of patience. The joke turns on "on leave" (a fern's leaves, plus
annual leave) and ends with a curt refusal to be watered, as if the
plant has filed its own out-of-office notice and closed the case.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

The program writes the single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
